# 🚀 MPLADS Risk Intelligence

This website performs AI-based monitoring, risk scoring (0–100), duplicate detection, and delay analysis for MPLADS works, enabling officers to identify high-risk projects and conduct timely physical verifications.

🔗 **Live Demo:**
[open live demo] https://rohitcoder47.github.io/SIH-2026-DEMO-Website/


---

## ✨ Features
AI Anomaly & Risk Detection (0–100 Score):
Automatically flags suspicious financial mismatches (e.g., 80% funds spent with only 20% ground work done), delay risks, and cost overruns with clear, explainable reasons.

GIS Map & Duplicate Project Detection:
Uses geo-coordinates to map all works and detect overlapping or duplicate projects sanctioned under other schemes (like PMGSY or Jal Jeevan Mission).

Role-Based Workflows (MP, Officer, Contractor):
Provides tailored dashboards—contractors upload geo-tagged photo proof, while District Officers inspect and verify high-risk sites.

Audit-Ready Reports & Field Inspection:
Turns raw project data into actionable field verification tasks and instant audit reports for Ministry and CAG compliance.
